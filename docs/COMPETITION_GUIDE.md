# **Google PromptWars Virtual — Competition & Challenge 6 Reference Guide**

**Program:** Build with AI — PromptWars Virtual ([PromptWars Virtual](https://promptwars.in/promptwarsVirtual.html))  
**Organizers:** Google for Developers & Hack2skill  
**Active Round:** Challenge 6 — Urban Air Quality Assistant

# **1\. Competition Overview & Eligibility**

* **Format:** Online, solo-participation, bi-weekly "vibe coding" (intent-driven AI development) competition structured across **8 challenges** in the season.  
* **Eligibility:** Open to individual builders in **India**, aged **18+**, including students, working developers, and AI enthusiasts. No teams or duos are permitted.  
* **Cost:** Free to participate.  
* **Core Philosophy:** Rather than writing every line manually, participants guide AI coding agents—specifically **Google Antigravity** and approved AI tools—using clear intent, architecture, and iterative prompting to ship working real-world web applications.

# **1.1 The 14-Day Sprint Cycle**

| Phase | Timeline | What Happens |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Phase 1: Challenge Release** | Start of bi-weekly cycle | Real-world problem statement is released (designed across Beginner, Intermediate, and Advanced depth). |
| **Phase 2: Building Phase** | Days 1–12 | Solo development using Google Antigravity and approved AI tools to build and deploy a live functional web app. |
| **Phase 3: Submission Phase** | By Day 13 | Two-part submission required: **(1) Technical** (code repository \+ live deployed preview) and **(2) Narrative** (LinkedIn "Build-in-Public" post / technical blog). |

# **2\. Challenge 6 Mandate: Urban Air Quality Assistant**

# **2.1 Official Problem Statement**

Create a GenAI-powered assistant that helps families and communities navigate severe winter smog and urban air pollution. The application should suggest low-emission daily routines, indoor air quality hacks, and sustainable alternatives tailored to their location and lifestyle, while clearly explaining the direct impact of their choices.

# **2.2 Core Functional Objectives**

Every Challenge 6 submission is expected to address six functional areas:

1. **Personalized Recommendations:** Guidance tailored to the user's specific location, household profile (e.g., children, elderly, respiratory sensitivity), and daily lifestyle.  
2. **Low-Emission Daily Routines:** Practical daily habits and commuting alternatives (such as carpooling, combining/chaining trips, or cleaner transit choices) that reduce individual contributions to air pollution.  
3. **Indoor Air Quality Hacks:** Actionable advice for improving indoor air quality (such as purifier usage/sizing, ventilation strategies, filtration hacks, or realistic indoor mitigation steps).  
4. **Real-Time Data Integration (Mandatory):** Must integrate live, real-time air quality data from reliable public sources (e.g., CPCB, OpenAQ, or equivalent public air quality APIs) rather than static datasets or AI-invented estimates.  
5. **User-Friendly Location Interaction:** Intuitive interface allowing users to easily input locations or pull location-based data automatically.  
6. **Justification of Action:** Clear reasoning behind every recommendation so users understand the direct health or environmental impact of their choices.

# **2.3 Technology Stack Policy**

* **No Fixed Stack:** Participants may use any web frameworks and AI tools they are comfortable with, as long as Generative AI is genuinely and effectively leveraged.  
* **Indicative Google Tools:** Google Antigravity (required/core IDE for PromptWars Virtual), Gemini, Cloud Run, Vertex AI, Firebase Studio, and other approved AI tools.

# **3\. Multi-Stage Evaluation & Validation Framework**

Submissions are judged through a three-part process covering code quality, live real-world utility, and authentic AI tool usage.

# **3.1 Stage 1: Initial Automated Code Assessment**

Triggered automatically upon submission to establish the initial leaderboard entry score across six technical signals:

| Technical Signal | What Is Checked |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Code Quality** | Clean structure, modularity, readability, and adherence to coding standards. |
| **Security** | Safe data handling, no exposed secrets/API keys, and vulnerability prevention. |
| **Efficiency** | Application performance, caching, and runtime optimization. |
| **Testing** | Robustness of the implementation and presence of automated test coverage. |
| **Accessibility** | Usability for all individuals (e.g., keyboard navigation, screen-reader support, non-color-only indicators). |
| **Problem Statement Alignment** | How directly and completely the codebase addresses Challenge 6 requirements. |

# **3.2 Stage 2: Manual Prototype Evaluation**

Conducted by the review team after the submission window closes:

* Verifies that every feature claimed in the documentation actually works in the **live, deployed application**.  
* Evaluates real-world utility, UX quality, and genuine GenAI integration.  
* **Leaderboard Impact:** Manual review can raise or lower a participant's ranking relative to the Stage 1 automated score.

# **3.3 Stage 3: Tool Usage Enforcement & Validation Mechanisms**

To ensure genuine adoption and learning rather than superficial one-shot usage, participants must document their workflow through:

* **Tool Usage Explanation in LinkedIn Post** ("Build-in-Public" narrative / technical write-up).  
* **Architecture and Prompt Flow Description** explaining how the system and AI pipeline operate.  
* **Optional Prompt Excerpts or Screenshots** showing actual build progression.

**Four Mandatory Disclosures (Tool Usage Enforcement):**

1. **Which tools were used** (e.g., Google Antigravity, Gemini, hosting/data tools).  
2. **Why they were selected** for the specific architecture.  
3. **How prompts evolved** across iterations.  
4. **What GenAI handled vs. what humans designed.**

# **4\. Leaderboard, Prompt Credits & Rewards**

Performance across the 8 challenges accumulates **Prompt Credits**, which can be redeemed in the Virtual Swag Store.

* **Cumulative Scoring:** Participants can join at any round, though competing across multiple challenges builds higher cumulative totals.  
* **Rank-Based Credit Examples:**  
  * **Rank 1:** 5,000 credits/challenge (30,000 over 6 challenges; 40,000 over 8 challenges — e.g., Premium Backpack, Desk Pad, Desk Organizer, Sweatshirt, Hoodie, Mobile Stand, Diary).  
  * **Rank 2:** 4,500 credits/challenge (27,000 over 6 challenges; 36,000 over 8 challenges — e.g., Premium Backpack, Desk Pad, Desk Organizer, Sweatshirt, Laptop Sleeve).  
  * **Rank 400:** 500 credits/challenge (4,000 over 8 challenges — e.g., Backpack or T-Shirt).  
* **Special Recognition:** Top 3 Women Developers per challenge receive 1,500 Prompt Credits.  
* **Certificates:** Official participation/recognition certificates are awarded.  
* **Redemption Deadline:** All Prompt Credits expire on **January 1, 2027** and must be redeemed before that date.

# **5\. Fair Play, Integrity & Disqualification Rules**

* **Strict Anti-Cheating Policy:** Google and Hack2skill prohibit plagiarism, copying submissions, cheating, or operating multiple accounts. Violations result in immediate termination and revocation of credits or prizes.  
* **Authentic AI Usage:** Submissions that fabricate live data, rely on static/mocked AQI instead of real-time public sources, or fail to genuinely integrate Generative AI risk failing prototype verification.  
* **Eligibility Exclusions:** Google employees, contractors, vendors, and their immediate family members are not eligible to win prizes.  
* **Organizer Discretion:** Shortlisting, verification, and final leaderboard decisions remain at the discretion of the PromptWars organizing committee.
